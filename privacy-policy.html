1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to AI Life Assistant ("the Application", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and maintained by NR Design. This Privacy Policy is designed to inform you about how our application handles information, user data, and privacy when you download, install, and use our Android mobile application (Package Name: com.aistudio.ailifeassistant.qmvztr).

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy provides a comprehensive, transparent explanation of our data collection practices, permissions, the local on-device architecture of the app, and our integration with third-party service providers including Google AdMob and the Google Gemini API.

Core Privacy Principle: Your personal tasks, daily reminders, personal finance records, bills, budgets, and travel plans are stored strictly locally on your Android device. We do not operate custom backend user databases that harvest, store, or sell your private personal content.

2. Information Collection & Practices

Depending on how you interact with the features inside AI Life Assistant, the following types of information are handled:

A. Information You Provide (Stored Locally On-Device)

The application allows you to organize your personal life through several productivity modules. All such data is saved directly in a private local database on your device (SQLite via Android Room):

  • Tasks and To-Do Items: Task titles, descriptions, priorities, categories, due dates, and completion statuses.
  • Reminders and Alarms: Reminder titles, scheduled trigger times, and alert intervals.
  • Personal Financial Logs: Transaction titles, amounts, income/expense classification, payment method labels (e.g. Cash, UPI, Card), and category tags.
  • Bills and Due Dates: Utility/service provider names, bill amounts, recurring frequencies, and payment due dates.
  • Budgets & Savings Goals: Monthly budget thresholds and financial savings targets.
  • Travel Itineraries: Saved trip plans, packing lists, and destination notes.

B. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs

When you use the app, third-party services—specifically Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)—may automatically collect certain technical and diagnostic data to serve advertisements, optimize ad delivery, and prevent fraud. This data may include:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID), Android ID, or unique vendor IDs.
  • Device & Hardware Details: Device brand and model, operating system version, system language, screen resolution, and carrier information.
  • Network Information: IP address (which may be used to approximate coarse geographic location for relevant advertising) and network connection type (Wi-Fi or Cellular).
  • Ad Interaction & Diagnostic Data: Ad views, clicks, impression duration, and diagnostic telemetry (such as crash logs and performance indicators).

3. Google AdMob & Advertising Disclosures

Our application uses Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to display banner advertisements. Advertising revenue helps support the ongoing development, maintenance, and free features of AI Life Assistant.

How Google AdMob Uses Data

Google AdMob uses device identifiers and technical data to deliver advertisements within the application. Depending on your device settings and applicable privacy regulations in your jurisdiction (such as GDPR, CCPA, or Google Play Families Policies):

  • Personalized Advertisements: Ads tailored to your interests based on previous app usage, advertising ID signals, and demographic approximations.
  • Non-Personalized Advertisements: Contextual ads that are not based on user profiles or past behavior, though identifiers are still utilized for frequency capping, aggregated ad reporting, and combating fraud and abuse.

Cookies and Mobile Identifiers

While our app is a native Android application rather than a web browser, Google AdMob uses mobile advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and internal software identifiers that function analogously to cookies to recognize your device across ad requests.

How to Opt-Out of Personalized Ads

You have full control over your mobile advertising preferences directly through your Android device settings:

  1. Open your device's Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID, or enable Opt out of Ads Personalization depending on your Android version.

To review Google's official privacy and advertising practices, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Device Permissions Used

AI Life Assistant requests only the minimum set of permissions necessary to deliver its core features. Below is an explanation of every permission declared in our AndroidManifest.xml:

Permission Type Purpose & Justification
android.permission.INTERNET Normal Allows the application to connect to the Internet to request banner ads from Google AdMob and process user queries with the Google Gemini AI API.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Enables the app to monitor whether an active network connection exists before making API calls or loading advertisement banners.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Used exclusively to deliver user-scheduled notifications for tasks, reminders, and upcoming bill due dates. You can revoke this permission anytime in Android Settings.
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal Provides haptic feedback and vibrations when an alarm or scheduled reminder notification triggers.
android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Normal Allows the app to reschedule your active task alarms and bill reminders automatically whenever your device reboots, ensuring no alerts are missed.

5. AI Features & Google Gemini API

AI Life Assistant includes an intelligent conversational assistant capable of helping you plan travel itineraries, summarize pending chores, and draft productivity schedules.

  • Processing Queries: When you enter a natural language prompt into the AI Assistant screen, the text of that specific query is sent securely over encrypted HTTPS to Google's generative AI endpoint (Gemini API) to generate a response.
  • No Sensitive Financial Data Sent: The app does not transmit sensitive banking account credentials, debit/credit card numbers, or passwords. Any calculations or summaries are performed on the device or based strictly on the query you choose to provide.
  • Privacy of AI Prompts: Information processed through the Gemini API is governed by Google's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

6. Data Storage & Local Retention

We believe in user data sovereignty. Unlike cloud-centralized applications, AI Life Assistant stores your personal information on your device:

  • Local Database: All tasks, financial transactions, recurring bills, trip plans, and savings goals reside in your device's private SQLite database (via Android Room), isolated by the Android OS application sandbox.
  • Retention Duration: Data remains on your device for as long as you choose to keep it or until you uninstall the app or clear the app's storage. We do not retain copies on external servers.

7. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal content or records to any third party.

Information is only shared under the following limited circumstances:

  • Third-Party Service Providers: With Google LLC through the integrated Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for ad serving, and through the Google Gemini API for fulfilling AI requests as described in this policy.
  • Legal Requirements: If compelled by lawful legal processes, court orders, or applicable governmental requests.
  • User-Initiated Sharing: When you explicitly choose to export or share a financial report or travel summary via Android's native system share sheet (e.g. sharing via messaging or email apps).

8. User Rights & Data Deletion

Because your personal records are stored locally on your device, you have immediate and complete control over your data at all times:

  • Access and Editing: You can view, modify, or update any task, transaction, reminder, or bill at any time directly in the app.
  • Individual Deletion: You can delete individual tasks, transactions, and bills via the trash icon in their respective screens.
  • Complete Data Erasure: To delete all local application data, you can:
    1. Open your device Settings → Apps → AI Life Assistant.
    2. Select Storage & cache.
    3. Tap Clear storage / Clear data.
    Alternatively, uninstalling the app immediately and permanently purges all databases and files associated with the application.
  • Permissions Management: You can revoke notification permissions at any time via Android Settings.

If you have questions regarding your data protection rights under regulations such as the GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or other regional privacy laws, please contact us at nihalnigam12@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

AI Life Assistant is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 where required by applicable local law). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children.

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), if we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete such data. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Data Security Measures

We implement standard industry security practices to protect your information:

  • Encrypted Communications: All network transmissions made by the application (including calls to Google APIs and AdMob servers) use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS).
  • OS Sandboxing: Android's application sandboxing prevents other installed apps from accessing AI Life Assistant's internal database files without explicit root compromise.
  • No Sensitive Plaintext Storage: The application does not store bank account passwords, PINs, or government identifiers.
